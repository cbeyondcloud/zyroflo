# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

It applies in every project space - issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews - and when
someone represents the project in public.

**Reporting.** Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainers at `sumit.gupta@cbeyond.com`. Every complaint is reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the person reporting is
respected.

**Enforcement** follows the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary
ban and permanent ban, according to the impact of the behaviour.
